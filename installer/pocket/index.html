Step 2 · on-car store

Install the MGU App Store

Then the car browses, installs and updates apps itself (Apps › Store). It needs internet for that, and the Store only starts after the head unit has restarted.

More sources for the Store

RideTube, cluster maps, iDrive 8 apps, yours

Everything here installs from Apps › Store on the car once the source is in the Store. Two of them are built in; the others are added here.

The Store lists nothing from a repo until it has a published Release with packaged apps (docs/MAKE-YOUR-OWN-APP.md). If it says the Store's updater is not running yet, restart the head unit and press again.
